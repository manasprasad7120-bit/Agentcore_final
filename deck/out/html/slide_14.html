Engagement path

From demonstration to a governed agent estate

Each stage reuses everything built in the previous one. Agents, roles, models and guardrails carry over unchanged.

Stage 1
Demonstrate
  • Platform running on a single machine, no cloud dependency
  • Underwriting agent on the bundled samples and your own documents
  • Hands-on session with your business and IT teams
  • Review of security, governance and integration questions
Outcome: A clear go or no-go on your first use case
Stage 2
Pilot
  • Deployed in your Azure tenant with Entra ID single sign-on
  • One business process built end to end with your experts
  • Governance, guardrails and tracing switched on from day one
  • Baseline measured: turnaround time, touch time, error rate
Outcome: One process live with measured results
Stage 3
Scale
  • Catalogue of approved agents, models, tools and guardrails
  • New processes reuse the same components and roles
  • Multi-region deployment and cost controls
  • Evaluation datasets drive continuous improvement
Outcome: A governed agent estate across the business
