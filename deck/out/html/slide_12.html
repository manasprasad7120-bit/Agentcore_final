Use case · Group medical insurance

Email-to-Quote: from a broker's inbox to a governed quote

An underwriting agent built entirely from AgentCore components. Documents in, an auditable decision and quote out.

Inputs
Broker emailEmployee census PDFClaims experience PDFCurrent policy PDFCompany application PDF
1
Intake
Reads the broker email and PDF attachments from the mailbox, an upload or a monitored folder.
2
Document intelligence
Extracts 20 underwriting fields with source document, page and confidence.
3
Field-level validation
Mandatory fields, formats, head-count and claims consistency. PASS, FAIL or WARN.
4
Underwriting decision
Proceed to quote, request clarification, or refer to an underwriter.
5
Rating engine
Deterministic premium from an editable rating table. The LLM never sets the price.
6
Quote and dispatch
Quote PDF and broker email, held as a draft until an underwriter approves.
Possible decisions Proceed to quote Request clarification Refer to underwriter Material validation failures put the case on hold.
Outputs
Underwriting decisionValidation tableExtracted data modelRating breakdownQuote PDFBroker email draftAudit trail
